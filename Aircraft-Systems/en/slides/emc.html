Slide 4: Definition of EMC

EMC is the ability of equipment to operate acceptably in its electromagnetic environment by limiting the generation, propagation and reception of unwanted electromagnetic energy that can cause EMI or physical damage. Sources: electrical equipment (generators, motors), radio transmitters, switching equipment.

Slide 6: External waves couple into equipment

External electromagnetic waves (radar, VHF/UHF transmitters) couple into wiring bundles, equipment and boards; external and internal (IC) interference cause malfunction and can lead to serious scenarios.

Slide 7: Electromagnetic environment of an aircraft

The environment includes lightning, atmosphere, solar and cosmic radiation, ground radars, radio stations, multipath reflections, internal systems (generators, avionics, switching) and other aircraft (TCAS). Four steps: unintended generation, propagation, reflection or scattering, reception.

Slide 10: Spectrum of a square wave

Rectangular pulse: v(t) = V sin(w0 t) + (V/3) sin(3 w0 t) + (V/5) sin(5 w0 t) + (V/7) sin(7 w0 t) + ...; only odd harmonics, amplitude falling as 1/n; the spectrum envelope is proportional to 1/n.

Slide 13: The decibel

L_P = 10 log10(P2/P1); L_A = 20 log10(A2/A1); 20 dB = amplitude x 10, 40 dB = x 100, 60 dB = x 1000. Examples: 1 mV to 1 V = 60 dB; 1 mW to 1 W = 30 dB; 10 uV/m to 1 mV/m = 40 dB; H from 1 to 0.1 A/m = -20 dB.

Summary transcript from an earlier read of the slide image (10/2026); numbers not yet rechecked at high zoom.

Formulas: L_P = 10 log10(P2/P1) · L_A = 20 log10(A2/A1)

10 W against 5 W is 3 dB (double). Table of power ratio and dB: 0.01 = -20; 0.1 = -10; 0.5 = -3; 1 = 0; 2 = 3; 10 = 10; 100 = 20.

Common mode: currents in the same direction, fluxes add, high impedance. Differential mode: opposite currents, fluxes cancel, low impedance. Saturation reduces the effect.

Slide 36: Real capacitor at high frequency

A real capacitor at high frequency is a series RLC (ESL, ESR): Z = R + jwL + 1/(jwC); resonant frequency f_r = 1/(2 pi sqrt(LC)); above f_r the capacitor behaves as an inductor; aircraft equipment uses more than 10 capacitors.
