Property tables lookup (Appendix 1, Cengel & Boles)

Look up water, R-134a, air and other gases, constants, fuels and isentropic flow from the tables printed in Appendix 1 (SI units; for air also the English-unit table A-17E). Every result comes with its source cell: table, printed page, the rows used and the page image so you can check it yourself.

ScopeLinear interpolation in the table's printed variable (superheated vapour: linear in T inside the two neighbouring pressure blocks, then linear in P; wet region: y = y_f + x·y_fg). Values outside the table are refused, never extrapolated. The charts (T-s, Mollier, P-h, psychrometric, generalized departure) are NOT read: out of scope. Substance names stay in English as printed in the tables.

Found by internal consistency checks (h − u = RT, Δs° = Δh/T, v_r·P_r/T = constant, h_fg = h_g − h_f…). The PRINTED number is still used in lookups; the "expected" column is a derived value, not a number from the book.

Reliability

Every table went through: structure checks; comparison with the page images by hand reading; internal consistency checks; comparison with iapws / CoolProp; re-running the worked examples of Tutorials I. Details: data/props/crosscheck.json, book_errata.json, handread.json.

NOT DONE (stated plainly)A-3, A-8, A-16, A-28 (equilibrium constants K_p), A-33, A-34 (normal shock, Rayleigh) and all English-unit tables except A-17E (A-1E…A-16E, A-18E…A-27E) were not extracted; the vector charts (A-9, A-10, A-14, A-15, A-29…A-31) are not read for values.
