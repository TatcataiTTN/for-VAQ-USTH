AE2.013 · Thermal Systems

Bilingual self-study system following the USTH syllabus, the lecturer's slides and the three references: gas power cycles, refrigeration and aircraft air conditioning, combustion, compressible flow. 4 ECTS · 40 h lecture · 12 h lab · 0 h tutorial (the syllabus says 0 h but the lecturer has a Tutorials I set) · prerequisite: AE1.030 Thermodynamics.

CO / CLO (syllabus)
  • CO1: Understand fundamental thermodynamic concepts and propulsion systems
  • CO2: Analyze thermal system components and processes
  • CO3: Apply mathematical and analytical tools to solve thermal system problems
  • CLO1: Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of thermodynamic principles and the classification of aerospace propulsion systems and engines.
  • CLO2: Apply mathematical models and computational tools to analyze and solve problems related to power cycles, combustion processes, and compressive flow in thermal systems.
  • CLO3: Evaluate the performance and efficiency of refrigeration cycles and air conditioning systems used in aircraft, including real and ideal processes.
  • CLO4: Identify, analyze, and balance combustion equations, and assess enthalpy changes in theoretical and practical combustion processes.
