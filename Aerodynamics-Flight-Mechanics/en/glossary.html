Bilingual glossary

Look up a concept in Vietnamese or English (accents optional). Every entry cites its source in EASA Module 08, Anderson or Etkin & Reid and the module that covers it in detail.

Atmospheric pressure Áp suất khí quyển

The force per unit area exerted by the weight of the air above; 14.7 psi = 1013.2 hPa at standard sea level.

EASA 1.2–1.3 · module 01

Barometer Khí áp kế

Instrument that measures atmospheric pressure, classically a mercury column read in inches of mercury.

EASA 1.2, Q1-1 · module 01

Density Mật độ

Mass per unit volume of air (kg/m³). Varies directly with pressure and inversely with temperature.

EASA 1.4; Anderson 1.4 · module 01

Equation of state (perfect gas) Phương trình trạng thái (khí lý tưởng)

p = ρRT; for air R = 287 J/(kg·K) = 1716 ft·lb/(slug·°R).

Anderson 1.1, 7.1 · module 01

Relative humidity Độ ẩm tương đối

Percentage ratio of the water vapour actually in the air to that at saturation at the same temperature and pressure.

EASA 1.4 · module 01

Troposphere / tropopause Tầng đối lưu / đỉnh đối lưu

Lowest layer where temperature falls about 2 °C per 1000 ft; its top, the tropopause, is about −57 °C. Most civil aviation flies here.

EASA 1.5, Q1-3 · module 01

Stratosphere Tầng bình lưu

Layer above the tropopause where temperature rises with altitude; contains the ozone layer.

EASA 1.5 · module 01

International Standard Atmosphere (ISA) Khí quyển chuẩn quốc tế (ISA)

Agreed model of the atmosphere (15 °C, 1013.25 hPa, 1.225 kg/m³ at sea level) used for calibration and performance comparison.

EASA 1.5–1.6; Etkin App. D · module 01

Density altitude Độ cao mật độ

The ISA altitude whose density equals the actual air density.

module 01 (suy ra từ ISA) · module 01

Archimedes buoyancy Lực đẩy Archimedes

Upward force equal to the weight of the displaced fluid.

Anderson 1.9, 1.12 · module 01

Velocity vs speed Vận tốc và tốc độ

Speed is the rate of motion; velocity is speed in a particular direction.

EASA 2.2, Q2-1 · module 02

Newton's laws of motion Các định luật Newton

1) inertia, 2) force equals rate of change of motion, 3) action and reaction.

EASA 2.2 · module 02

Bernoulli's principle Nguyên lý Bernoulli

In steady inviscid incompressible flow p + ½ρV² is constant along a streamline: faster flow, lower pressure.

EASA 2.3, Q2-2; Anderson 3.2 · module 02

Dynamic pressure Áp suất động

q = ½ρV².

Anderson 1.4 · module 02

Total (stagnation) pressure Áp suất toàn phần (đình trệ)

Pressure obtained when the flow is brought to rest isentropically; what a Pitot tube senses.

Anderson 3.4, 7.5 · module 02

Stagnation point Điểm đình trệ

Point on the body where the local velocity is zero.

EASA 2.3 · module 02

Free stream Dòng tự do

Air flowing without obstruction before it meets the aircraft.

EASA 2.3 · module 02

Boundary layer Lớp biên giới

The part of the airflow closest to the surface, retarded by viscosity (laminar, turbulent or separated).

EASA 2.4, Q2-3 · module 02

Flow separation Tách dòng

Detachment of the flow from the surface; produces no lift and underlies the stall.

EASA 2.3–2.4 · module 02

Chord Dây cung

Distance from the leading edge to the trailing edge of the wing.

EASA Q2-4 · module 02

Camber Độ cong

Curvature of the mean line of the airfoil.

EASA 2.8 · module 02

Aspect ratio (AR) Tỉ số kích thước (độ dài mảnh)

Span over chord; for any wing b²/S.

EASA Q2-5; Anderson 5.1 · module 02

Mean aerodynamic chord (MAC) Dây cung khí động trung bình

Chord of an equivalent rectangular wing with the same aerodynamic behaviour.

EASA 2.7; Etkin App. C · module 02

Angle of attack (AOA) Góc tấn

Angle between the chord line and the relative wind.

EASA 2.11, Q2-6 · module 02

Lift coefficient C_L Hệ số lực nâng

L = ½ρV²·S·C_L; depends on airfoil shape and angle of attack.

EASA 2.11; Anderson 1.5 · module 02

Drag coefficient C_D Hệ số lực cản

D = ½ρV²·S·C_D.

EASA 2.11 · module 02

Parasite drag Lực cản ký sinh

Drag not produced by lift (form, skin friction, interference); increases continuously with speed.

EASA 2.14, Q2-8 · module 02

Interference drag Lực cản nhiễu

Drag from the mixing of streamlines between components such as wing and fuselage.

EASA Q2-7 · module 02

Induced drag Lực cản cảm ứng

Drag that is an inevitable consequence of lift, caused by downwash: C_D,i = C_L²/(π e AR).

EASA 2.14; Anderson 5.3 · module 05

Downwash / upwash Luồng đi xuống / đi lên

Downward deflection of air behind the wing / upward deflection ahead of it.

EASA 2.3; Anderson 5.1 · module 05

Wing-tip vortex Xoáy đầu cánh

Vortex formed where high-pressure air under the wing curls round the tip.

EASA 2.7; Anderson 5.1 · module 05

Center of pressure (CP) Tâm áp lực

Point on the airfoil where the resultant aerodynamic force acts.

Anderson 1.6 · module 02

Aerodynamic center Tâm khí động

Point about which the pitching moment is independent of angle of attack; near c/4 for NACA 2412 (x = 0.242c).

Anderson 4.9, Bài 4.10 · module 02

Circulation Lưu số

Line integral of velocity round a closed curve; lift per unit span L′ = ρ V Γ (Kutta–Joukowski).

Anderson 3.16, 4.4 · module 02

Thin airfoil theory Lý thuyết cánh mỏng

Linear theory giving c_l = 2π(α − α_L=0) and quarter-chord aerodynamic centre.

Anderson 4.7–4.8 · module 02

Kutta condition Điều kiện Kutta

Flow leaves the sharp trailing edge smoothly, which fixes the circulation.

Anderson 4.5 · module 02

Biot–Savart law Định luật Biot–Savart

Velocity induced by a vortex filament: dV = Γ/(4π) · dl × r / |r|³.

Anderson 5.2 · module 05

Lifting-line theory Lý thuyết đường nâng

Prandtl's model of a finite wing as a lifting line with a trailing vortex sheet.

Anderson 5.3 · module 05

Span efficiency factor e Hệ số hiệu suất sải cánh

e = 1/(1+δ); e = 1 for an elliptical lift distribution.

Anderson 5.3 · module 05

Winglet Cánh nhỏ đầu cánh (winglet)

Small surface at the wing tip that weakens the tip vortex and reduces induced drag.

EASA 2.14, Hình 2-20 · module 05

Wash-in / wash-out Tăng / giảm góc đặt cánh về đầu cánh

Wing incidence increasing / decreasing towards the tip.

EASA syllabus 8.2 · module 02

Fineness ratio Tỉ số mảnh

Ratio of body length to its maximum diameter.

EASA syllabus 8.2 · module 02

Thrust / weight / lift / drag Lực đẩy / trọng lượng / lực nâng / lực cản

The four forces; steady level flight has L = W and T = D.

EASA 3.2, Q3-1, Q3-2 · module 03

Glide ratio Tỉ số lướt

Distance travelled per unit of height lost with power off; equals L/D in a steady glide.

EASA 3.3 · module 03

Polar curve Đường cực

Plot of C_L against C_D; the tangent from the origin gives the maximum L/D.

EASA 3.5 · module 03

Load factor Hệ số tải

Ratio of the load on the structure to the weight, in g; n = 1/cos φ in a level turn.

EASA 3.9, Q3-4 · module 03

Stall Thất tốc

Loss of lift when the angle of attack exceeds the critical value and flow separates.

EASA 3.7 · module 03

Flight envelope Biên dạng bay (phạm vi bay)

Permitted combinations of speed and load factor.

EASA 3.9 · module 03

Flap Cánh tà

Trailing-edge high-lift device (plain, split, slotted, Fowler); raises lift and drag.

EASA 3.16, Q3-7 · module 03

Slat / leading-edge devices Cánh trước (slat) / thiết bị mép trước

Devices at the leading edge that keep the flow attached at high angle of attack.

EASA 3.17 · module 03

Vortex generator Tấm tạo xoáy

Small airfoil on the upper surface that energises the boundary layer.

EASA 3.18, Q3-8 · module 03

Mach number Số Mach

Ratio of true airspeed to the speed of sound.

EASA Q3-5 · module 03

Speed of sound Tốc độ âm thanh

a = √(γRT): 661 kt at sea level ISA, 574 kt at 40 000 ft.

EASA 3.11; Anderson 8.3 · module 03

Critical Mach number Số Mach tới hạn

Free-stream Mach number at which sonic flow first appears somewhere on the wing.

EASA 3.11; Anderson 11.6 · module 03

Compressibility Tính nén được

Change of air density with pressure; important at high speed.

EASA 3.10, Q3-6 · module 03

Sweepback Cánh xuôi sau

Wing sweep that delays the onset of compressibility effects.

EASA 3.14, Q3-6 · module 03

Total temperature T₀ Nhiệt độ toàn phần T₀

T₀/T = 1 + (γ−1)M²/2.

Anderson 7.5, 8.4 · module 06

Isentropic flow Dòng đẳng entropi

Reversible adiabatic flow; p/ρ^γ constant.

Anderson 7.2, 8.4 · module 06

Normal shock wave Sóng xung kích thẳng

Thin wave normal to the flow turning supersonic flow subsonic; p, T, ρ jump, p₀ falls, T₀ unchanged.

Anderson 8.2 · module 06

Rayleigh Pitot formula Công thức Pitot Rayleigh

Relation between Pitot pressure behind a bow shock and static pressure to find supersonic M.

Anderson 8.7 · module 06

Oblique shock Sóng xung kích xiên

Shock inclined at wave angle β to the flow, turning it by θ; acts as a normal shock for M₁ sin β.

Anderson 9.2 · module 07

θ–β–M relation Quan hệ θ–β–M

Relation between flow deflection θ, wave angle β and Mach number; has a maximum θ.

Anderson 9.3 · module 07

Detached shock Sóng tách rời

Curved shock ahead of the body when θ exceeds θ_max.

Anderson 9.5 · module 07

Mach angle Góc Mach

μ = arcsin(1/M).

Anderson 9.1 · module 07

Prandtl–Meyer expansion Giãn nở Prandtl–Meyer

Isentropic expansion fan round a convex corner; θ = ν(M₂) − ν(M₁).

Anderson 9.6 · module 07

Shock–expansion theory Lý thuyết sóng xung kích–giãn nở

Method for supersonic airfoils using oblique shocks and expansion fans.

Anderson 9.7 · module 07

Longitudinal / lateral / vertical axis Trục dọc / ngang / đứng

Nose-to-tail, wingtip-to-wingtip and top-to-bottom axes about which roll, pitch and yaw occur.

EASA 4.2, Q4-1 · module 04

Static stability Ổn định tĩnh

Initial tendency after a disturbance: positive, neutral or negative.

EASA 4.3 · module 04

Dynamic stability Ổn định động

Response over time: amplitude decreasing, constant or growing.

EASA 4.4 · module 04

Longitudinal stability Ổn định dọc

Stability in pitch about the lateral axis; depends on wing and tail position relative to the CG and tail area.

EASA 4.4, Q4-2 · module 04

Neutral point Điểm trung hòa

CG position at which C_mα = 0; the CG must lie ahead of it.

Etkin 2.3 · module 04

Static margin K_n Lề ổn định tĩnh

K_n = h_n − h; positive means statically stable.

Etkin 2.3 · module 04

Dihedral Góc V ngang

Upward inclination of the wings; increases lateral stability.

EASA 4.7, Q4-5 · module 08

Directional (weathercock) stability Ổn định hướng

Tendency to turn the nose into the relative wind, from the fin; yawing stability.

EASA 4.8, Q4-6; Etkin 3.9 · module 08

Phugoid Phugoid

Long-period, lightly damped longitudinal oscillation exchanging height and speed (B747: T = 93 s).

Etkin 6.2 · module 04

Short-period mode Mode chu kỳ ngắn

Fast, heavily damped pitching oscillation (B747: T = 7.1 s).

Etkin 6.2 · module 04

Eigenvalue Trị riêng

Root λ = σ ± iω of the characteristic equation; σ < 0 stable.

Etkin 6.1 · module 04

Damping ratio ζ Hệ số tắt dần

ζ = −σ/|λ|.

Etkin 6.2 · module 04

Dutch roll Dutch roll

Coupled yaw–roll oscillation, lightly damped on the B747 (ζ = 0.035).

EASA 4.9; Etkin 6.7 · module 08

Spiral mode Mode xoắn ốc

Very slow lateral mode (B747 t½ = 95 s).

Etkin 6.7–6.8 · module 08

Roll convergence Hội tụ lăn

Fast non-oscillatory lateral mode (B747 t½ = 1.2 s).

Etkin 6.7 · module 08

Yaw damper Bộ giảm xoay

Feedback of yaw rate to the rudder to add damping to the Dutch roll.

Etkin 8.7 · module 08

Stability derivative Đạo hàm ổn định

Partial derivative of a force or moment coefficient with respect to a motion variable (e.g. C_mα, C_nβ).
