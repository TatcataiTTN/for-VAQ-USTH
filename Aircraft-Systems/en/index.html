AE2.021 · Electronics Systems: Analog Systems and Digital Systems

Bilingual self-study system following the USTH syllabus, the lecturer's 342 slides (9 decks) and the two textbooks that are available (Tooley, Floyd): number systems, logic gates and Boolean algebra, integrated circuits, computers and microprocessors, data buses, electronic displays, aircraft software, fibre optics, EMC. 4 ECTS · 50 h lecture · 8 h practice (the syllabus does not describe it) · 0 h tutorial · prerequisite: AE2.025 Electronics 2.

CO / CLO (syllabus)
  • CO1: To provide students with Basic knowledge on the operations of analog systems and digital systems in aircraft
  • CO2: Convert numbers between decimal, binary, octal, and hexadecimal number systems, explain the operation of digital logic gates.
  • CO3: Use Boolean algebra to express logic operations as a equations, use Karnaugh maps to minimize Boolean equations
  • CO4: Understand the structure and operation of data conversion circuit and semiconductor memories
  • CO5: Define and describe the basic operation of computer system, microprocessor, and microcontroller
  • CLO1: providing an overview of flight instruments and cockpit layouts
  • CLO2: describes the fabrication technology and application of a wide variety of modern integrated circuits
  • CLO3: introduction to a variety of different avionic systems based on digital electronics and computers
  • CLO4: provides an introduction to electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) and provides examples of measures that can be taken to both reduce EMI and improve EMC
