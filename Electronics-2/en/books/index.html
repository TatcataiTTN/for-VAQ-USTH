How to read
  • Printed page is the number printed on the book page; PDF page is the page number inside the PDF file (Floyd: PDF = printed + 18; Jaeger: + 29; EASA: PDF = sub-module printed page + 11, + 87, + 101 for SM01/SM02/SM03).
  • Floyd and Jaeger page numbers come from the PDF's own bookmarks, cross-checked with the printed Brief Contents. The EASA contents were typed from the book's printed contents and every heading was searched again in the text of its PDF page. SERVO is read from the numbered headings in the file.
  • Section titles stay in the books' English so they are easy to look up; only chapter titles carry a Vietnamese gloss.
  • Coverage: Floyd 54/145 sections used by a module; Jaeger 35/274 sections mapped (reading references only, not read and no questions quoted); EASA 78/127 headings used by a module; SERVO: all of it belongs to m10.
Role legend
  • core Core: the lesson and/or the questions are built from this section
  • lab Used for the servomotor lab (m10)
  • ref Reading reference from the syllabus map: mapped, NOT quoted
  • cited Questions or lesson mention this section
  • optional Chapter feature (Application Activity, Programmable Analog Technology): not used by the lessons
  • background background (discrete-device theory, previous course)
  • deeper same chapter, deeper than required
  • outside syllabus outside the syllabus
