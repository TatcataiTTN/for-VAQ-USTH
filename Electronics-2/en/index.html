AE2.025 · Electronics 2

Bilingual self-study system following the USTH syllabus: integrated circuits (IC), printed circuit boards (PCB), servomechanisms and their aircraft applications. 4 ECTS · 30 h lecture · 9 h tutorial · 24 h lab · prerequisite: AE1.033 Electronics 1.

Course learning outcomes
  • CLO1: Clearly state and illustrate the principles of IC, PCB and servomechanism
  • CLO2: Apply maths and physics to analyze and interpret equations related to functions of the electronic circuits
  • CLO3: Analyze and calculate parameters of each electronic component used to build a specific electronic circuit
  • CLO4: Work in group to build a circuit to control a servomotor
