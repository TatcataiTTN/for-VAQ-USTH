What the books do not have

ECS / cooling pack: no book has it

  • Slide Ch.3 p.29–36 (p.29 “Air conditioner in aircraft”; p.30 “AIR CYCLE COOLING PACK”; p.31 “Operation of the Cooling Pack” …) has no counterpart in Cengel, Farokhi or El-Sayed.
  • Farokhi: searched for air conditioning, ECS, cooling pack, air cycle, customer bleed, cabin, refrigeration: no section. Only rocket propellant-tank pressurisation (Ch.12), turbine-cooling bleed and inlet boundary-layer bleed turn up, none of them cabin air.
  • El-Sayed: only passing mentions, no description of an ECS, air cycle machine or cooling pack: printed p.301 (Ch.5); printed p.13 (Ch.1); printed p.500 (Ch.6). The sentence on printed p.301 only says bleed “has its applications for cabin-air conditioning” and lies outside the scope chapters.
  • Nearest section: Cengel §11-8 Gas Refrigeration Cycles (reversed Brayton cycle, printed p.628, PDF 654), plus Cengel Ch.14 §14-7 (air-conditioning processes). No book describes the aircraft cooling pack, so this part of m03 rests on the slides alone.

Table-of-contents discrepancies found

  • Cengel, the Contents pages (PDF 2–17) number Ch.6 off by one: 6–4 is skipped, so entries 6–5…6–12 in the Contents are 6–4…6–11 in the body (the last, “The Carnot Refrigerator and Heat Pump”, is 6–11 at printed p.309, PDF 335). The map uses the body numbers.
  • Cengel Ch.12: section 12–4 “General Relations” is in the body (printed p.661, PDF 687) but missing from the Contents; its title comes from the body heading.
  • Cengel: some section names in the Contents differ in capitalisation or spacing from the body (for example “Brayton Cycle: The Ideal…”); the map uses the Contents names.
  • El-Sayed: the ⟦p.N⟧ marks in the OCR text sometimes lag a few lines behind the EPUB page-break marks; the map uses the page breaks in the xhtml (230/230 entries match).
